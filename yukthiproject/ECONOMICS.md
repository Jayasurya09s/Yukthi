# Business Economics

## Objective

The goal of Yukthi is to create a scalable AI infrastructure optimization platform with strong operational leverage and recurring organizational value.

The platform combines:
- automated audits
- optimization intelligence
- lead generation
- recurring infrastructure visibility

---

# Cost Structure

## Current Infrastructure Costs

### 1. OpenRouter API Costs

Purpose:
- AI-generated executive summaries

Current usage:
- lightweight summarization only
- deterministic calculations handled internally

Estimated cost characteristics:
- low token usage
- short responses
- low operational overhead

Reason for architecture:
- minimizing LLM dependency reduces operational cost volatility

---

### 2. Supabase Costs

Purpose:
- audit persistence
- lead storage
- public report retrieval

Current usage:
- lightweight relational storage
- small structured payloads

Cost expectation:
- low early-stage operational costs

---

### 3. Resend Email Costs

Purpose:
- audit delivery
- shareable report emails

Usage:
- transactional email delivery

Expected cost profile:
- low initial email volume
- scalable operational pricing

---

### 4. Vercel Hosting Costs

Purpose:
- frontend hosting
- API routes
- public share pages

Expected cost characteristics:
- low early-stage infrastructure requirements
- globally distributed delivery

---

- scalability
