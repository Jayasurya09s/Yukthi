# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Product Positioning

Yukthi is positioned as:
- an AI infrastructure optimization platform
- a financial intelligence layer for AI tooling
- a SaaS spend-efficiency system

The platform targets organizations rapidly adopting AI tools without structured cost visibility.

---

# Problem Being Solved

Modern startups increasingly use:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- Copilot
- Gemini
- OpenAI APIs
- Anthropic APIs

As adoption grows:
- subscriptions overlap
- enterprise plans become unnecessary
- teams lose visibility into AI spend efficiency

Most teams currently manage AI costs through:
- spreadsheets
- manual reviews
- fragmented finance workflows

Yukthi aims to simplify this process through automated optimization analysis.
