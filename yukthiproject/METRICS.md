# Product Metrics

## Product Goal

The primary goal of Yukthi is to help startups and engineering teams identify and reduce unnecessary AI infrastructure spending.

The platform focuses on:
- optimization discovery
- financial visibility
- lead generation
- shareable audit workflows

---

# North Star Metric

## Primary Metric

```txt
Completed Yukthiss
```

Why:
- audits represent meaningful product engagement
- audits trigger the optimization engine
- audits drive lead generation
- audits create shareable reports

---

# Secondary Metrics

## 1. Lead Capture Conversion Rate

Definition:
```txt
% of audit users who submit email information
```

Importance:
- validates business interest
- measures report-save intent
- supports future monetization

---

## 2. Audit Completion Rate

Definition:
```txt
Users who complete the full audit flow
```

Flow:
```txt
Landing Page
→ Spend Form
→ Results Dashboard
```

Importance:
- measures onboarding clarity
- validates UX effectiveness

---

## 3. Public Share Rate

Definition:
```txt
% of audits shared publicly
```

Importance:
- measures virality potential
- indicates perceived value
- supports organic growth loops

---

## 4. Average Estimated Savings

Definition:
```txt
Average projected annual savings identified per audit
```

Importance:
- demonstrates platform value
- supports marketing positioning
- improves conversion potential

---

## 5. Returning Users

Definition:
```txt
Users performing multiple audits
```

Importance:
- indicates trust
- validates product usefulness
- supports future retention strategy

# Product Philosophy

The platform prioritizes:
- meaningful audits
- deterministic trust
- shareability
- actionable recommendations

over vanity metrics such as:
- page views
- raw traffic
- superficial engagement

The core belief is that:
```txt
High-quality optimization insights create stronger long-term product value than superficial AI interactions.
```
